# Iantrek

> Iantrek, Inc. is an ophthalmic medical technology company in White Plains, New York, pioneering
> bio-interventional ophthalmic surgery (BIOS) for glaucoma. Founded in 2019 by Sean Ianchulev, MD, MPH,
> it develops micro-interventional devices that reinforce and restore the eye's natural aqueous outflow
> pathways with biologic allograft tissue rather than bypassing or removing tissue. Iantrek publishes a
> corporate and surgeon-facing website only — it operates **no public API, developer portal, documentation
> site, or machine-readable contract** of any kind as of 2026-08-04.

## Products

- [AlloFlo Uveo](https://iantrekmed.com/alloflo): Bio-interventional treatment targeting the uveoscleral outflow pathway; US commercial launch October 2025.
- [AlloSert Uveo](https://iantrekmed.com/allosert): Allograft delivery/insertion system for the uveoscleral pathway.
- [C-Rex](https://iantrekmed.com/crex): Micro-interventional device used in canal-based procedures.
